# Third-party components

This project uses the following third-party implementations and libraries.
Their original source notices and applicable licenses remain in effect.

- `model/model_superhuman/` and `model/model_superhuman2.py`: Superhuman-style anisotropic segmentation blocks.
- `model/psrt_sliding_arch.py`: Swin/PSRT-derived feature fusion.
- `data/augmentation/` and affinity utilities: inherited augmentation and affinity implementations, including PyTorch Connectomics utilities.
- `third_party/fragment.py`: watershed and label-processing utilities from the original project.
- PyTorch, BasicSR, MMCV, timm, Waterz and other dependencies retain their respective licenses.
